About

I enjoy building things, from low-level hardware and embedded systems to AI-powered applications.

Previously, I worked on open-source hardware, AI agents, and consumer products. Through building AI companions, I became increasingly interested in a deeper question: can AI understand not just what people say, but what they feel, why they feel it, and the social context behind their behavior?

I'm currently studying AI fundamentals and exploring research in affective computing and computational emotion modeling.

Research Interests

My central interest is understanding how we can build AI systems that better model human emotions, psychological motivations, and social relationships.

I'm especially interested in:

  • Affective Computing — Modeling and understanding human emotions through computational methods.
  • Computational Emotion Modeling — Exploring representations of emotion and how they relate to psychological theories.
  • Social Cognition & Human-AI Interaction — Understanding how AI can reason about human intentions, relationships, and social contexts.
  • LLM Interpretability — Investigating how emotional and social information is represented within language models.

One question I'm particularly interested in:

How can we move beyond emotion recognition toward a deeper computational understanding of human experience?
